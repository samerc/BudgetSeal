BudgetSeal Help Guide

Everything you need to know about managing your budget with BudgetSeal.

What is BudgetSeal?

BudgetSeal is an envelope budgeting app. The idea is simple: every dollar you earn gets a job. You divide your money into virtual envelopes — groceries, rent, entertainment — and spend from them. When an envelope is empty, you've reached your limit for that category.

The app works entirely offline. Your data lives on your device, not on a server. You can optionally sync to Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox if you want a backup or to share with a partner.

The basic flow:
  1. Add accounts — where your money lives (bank, cash, credit card)
  2. Create envelopes — one for each spending category
  3. Fund envelopes — distribute your income into envelopes
  4. Spend — each expense automatically draws from its envelope

Setting Up

When you open BudgetSeal for the first time, a 3-step setup walks you through everything:

Step 1: Welcome

You'll see a quick overview of how envelope budgeting works. From here you can:

  • Get Started — set up a new household
  • Restore from Cloud — restore an existing backup from Google Drive or a file
  • Join a Household — enter an invite code to join someone else's BudgetSeal

Step 2: Your Household

Configure the basics:

  • Household name — a label for your budget (e.g., "My Budget" or "Family")
  • Base currency — the main currency you use (USD, EUR, etc.). It starts on your phone's region currency
  • Period start day — when your monthly budget cycle starts (1st, 15th, etc.)
  • First account — name, type (Cash/Bank/Credit/Digital), and opening balance

Under More options (tap to expand), you can also customize:

  • Categories — start with a full set of 30 categories, or create your own from scratch
  • Entry mode — choose Assisted (step-by-step) or Classic (all fields at once)
You can change all of these later in Settings. Don't overthink it — just pick what feels right and adjust as you go.

Step 3: Done

You're ready to go! The app opens to your dashboard.

Dashboard

The Home tab shows your financial overview at a glance. It's designed to be clean and scannable — the large title shrinks into a compact bar as you scroll:

  • Quick Actions — buttons for Expense, Income, Transfer, Fund, and Split (at the top for fast access)
  • Spending Overview — donut chart showing where your money went, with income/expense/net stats (toggle between last 7 days and this month)
  • Upcoming — total and count of recurring bills due in the next 7 days (an Overdue box appears next to it if a bill is late). Tap to open Upcoming Bills.
  • Your Money — compact card showing net worth and unallocated balance side by side. Tap net worth to go to Accounts, tap unallocated to fund envelopes.
  • Recent Activity — quick templates and latest transactions

To customize what appears on your dashboard:

  2. Drag sections to reorder them
  3. Toggle sections on/off
  4. Tap Reset to restore the default layout
Detailed stats like spending velocity, age of money, and envelope health are in the Reports tab → Insights. The dashboard focuses on quick overview and fast actions.

Accounts

Accounts represent where your money physically lives — a bank account, your wallet, a credit card, or a digital wallet (PayPal, Venmo, etc.).

Creating an Account

  1. Go to More tab → tap Accounts
  2. Tap the + button
  3. Enter a name (e.g., "Chase Checking")
  4. Choose a type: Cash, Bank, Credit card, or Digital wallet
  5. Set the currency and opening balance
  6. Tap Create

Account Detail

Tap any account to see its transactions and manage it. From the 3-dot menu, you can:

  • Reconcile balance — enter the balance your bank shows. You see the difference before confirming; if it matches, the account is simply marked reconciled, otherwise an adjustment is recorded. The balance card shows when you last reconciled
  • See all (next to Recent transactions) — the Activity tab filtered to this account
  • Archive — hide the account (transactions are preserved). The balance must be zero first: transfer what's left to another account or adjust the balance, since archived accounts no longer count toward your money
  • Convert Back & Close — for travel wallets, return remaining money and archive

Archived Accounts

When you archive an account, it disappears from the main list but its transactions are preserved. To see or restore archived accounts:

  1. On the Accounts screen, tap the 3-dot menu (top-right)
  2. Tap Show Archived
  3. Archived accounts appear in a separate section at the bottom, dimmed
  4. Tap the unarchive icon next to an account to restore it
  5. Tap the menu again → Hide Archived to hide the section

Decimal Precision

Some currencies use 0 decimal places (JPY, KRW), others use 3 (BHD, KWD). BudgetSeal auto-detects this based on the currency. You can override it per account if needed:

  1. Open the account → 3-dot menu → Edit
  2. Change the Decimal Places dropdown (Auto, 0, 1, 2, or 3)
Screenshot: Accounts list showing bank, cash, and credit card accounts with balances

Transactions

Every time money moves — you spend, earn, or transfer — you record a transaction. BudgetSeal supports three types:

TypeWhat it does
ExpenseMoney leaves an account (and its linked envelope)
IncomeMoney enters an account (goes to Unallocated)
TransferMoney moves between two accounts

Two Entry Modes

Assisted mode (default) walks you through step by step. A progress indicator (dots) shows which step you're on:

  1. Step 1 — Choose expense/income/transfer, then enter a title (the app may auto-detect the category)
  2. Step 2 — Pick or confirm the category
  3. Step 3 — Enter the amount, select an account, and tap Save

Classic mode shows all fields on one screen — useful for complex entries with multiple items, notes, or foreign currencies.

To switch modes: go to More → Settings & Customization → Entry Mode.

Faster Logging

  • Last account — a new transaction starts on the account you used last (transfers remember both accounts). Auto-fill can still switch it when you pick a category
  • Recent categories — the category picker shows your most recent categories at the top, and opens on the type you're entering
  • Today / Yesterday — tap the chip beside the date
  • Past months — adding from a past month on the Activity tab dates the transaction in that month
  • Save & new — in classic mode, saves and clears the form for the next one, keeping the date and account
  • Undo — the "Saved" message has an Undo button for a few seconds
  • Calculator — when it opens on an amount, the amount is highlighted: typing replaces it, an operator or ⌫ edits it
  • Quick add — type "Coffee 4.50" (or "4,50") in the bar at the bottom of the Activity tab
  • Shortcuts — long-press the app icon for Add transaction, Fund envelopes and Upcoming bills. Tapping the daily reminder opens a new transaction
  • Leaving the form with unsaved changes asks before discarding them. In assisted mode you can edit the title from the amount step and remove an item by tapping × on its chip

Adding a Transaction (Classic Mode)

  1. Tap the + button on the Activity tab (opens expense form directly; long-press for income or transfer)
  2. Choose Expense, Income, or Transfer
  3. Tap the category icon in the colored header to pick a category (recent ones are at the top)
  4. Tap the amount in the header to enter it with the calculator
  5. Fill in the title, date (or tap Today/Yesterday) and optional note — they share one card
  6. Check the account — it starts on the one you used last
  7. Tap Add transaction, or Save & new to log another right away

Multi-Item Transactions (Splits)

Need to split a grocery receipt across categories? Use the "Add item" button to add multiple line items, each with its own amount, category, and optionally a different account.

Transfers Between Accounts

  1. Choose Transfer as the type
  2. Select the From and To accounts (⇅ swaps them)
  3. Enter the amount
  4. If the accounts are in different currencies, an exchange rate field appears — enter how much the destination receives

Duplicate Detection

Before saving, BudgetSeal checks if a transaction with the same amount, category, and date already exists. If a possible duplicate is found, a dialog shows the existing transaction's details so you can decide whether to save or cancel.

Auto-fill

When you pick a category, BudgetSeal can automatically fill in the account, title, and amount from your last transaction with that category. Configure what gets auto-filled in Settings & Customization → Auto-fill.

Receipt Scanning

In Classic mode, tap the camera icon next to the title field to scan a receipt. The app uses offline OCR to extract text — no internet required. You can also attach receipt photos to any transaction.

Duplicate Detection

If you try to save a transaction with the same amount, category, and date as an existing one, BudgetSeal will warn you. You can still save it if it's intentional.

Managing Transactions

  • Edit — swipe right on a transaction, or tap it and then the edit icon. Receipts stay attached when you edit
  • Select — long-press a transaction to select it, then tap others to add them to the selection
  • Delete — swipe left on a transaction, delete it from its detail screen, or select one or more and tap the delete icon. You get a few seconds to tap Undo
  • Duplicate — select a single transaction and tap the copy icon (or ⋮ → Duplicate on its detail screen). Splits and transfers are copied with all their lines and accounts
  • No rate — an amber tag on a transaction means a foreign-currency amount was saved without an exchange rate, so totals and reports leave it out. Open it and add a rate, or set one in Settings → Exchange rates
  • Search — tap the search icon on the Activity tab. Searching (or setting a date range) looks through every month, not just the one open
  • Filter — by type (All/Income/Expense/Transfer), account, category (includes its subcategories), date range, or amount range (1,200 works). Active filters show as chips under the month tabs: tap × to remove one, or Clear all
  • See all — on an account or envelope, opens the Activity tab filtered to it across all months
  • Bulk changes — select transactions, then ⋮ for Select all, Change category, Change account, Change date or Export CSV. Envelope balances update; transfers only take a new date
  • Open from anywhere — tap a row in an envelope's activity, a transaction in a Reports category list, or a donut slice to drill in. The year picker goes back to your first transaction

Envelopes (Budgeting)

Envelopes are the heart of BudgetSeal. They don't move money between accounts — they help you plan how to use the money you already have.

Think of it like putting cash into labeled envelopes: $500 for groceries, $1,200 for rent, $100 for entertainment. When you spend, the money comes out of the right envelope.

Two Types of Envelopes

TypeUse caseBehavior
SpendingGroceries, fuel, diningSet a monthly budget. Resets each period.
RolloverAnything elseUnspent money rolls over to the next period. Set an optional target or leave open.
Envelopes plan how to use the money you already have — they don't move money between accounts. To save toward a specific goal (vacation, emergency fund) or track debts, use Goals & Loans instead. Goals create real transactions that move money.

Creating an Envelope

  1. Go to the Budget tab
  2. Tap the + button
  3. Enter a name and optionally pick an emoji icon
  4. Choose Spending or Rollover
  5. Set a budget amount (spending) or optional target (rollover)
  6. Pick a currency
  7. Link categories — expenses in these categories will automatically debit this envelope
  8. Tap Create Envelope

Envelope Settings

Tap an envelope, then use the 3-dot menu → Edit Settings to change:

  • Periodic vs Permanent — periodic resets each month; permanent accumulates
  • Rollover balance — carry remaining funds to the next period
  • Auto-reset — reset automatically at period start, or manually review

Envelope Detail Screen

Tap any envelope to see:

  • Balance card — how much is available, with a progress bar
  • Fund button — add money from your unallocated pool
  • Recent activity — funded, spent, adjustments, and resets
  • Spending history — a chart of past periods

The 3-dot menu also has:

  • Move money — move money to another envelope or back to Ready to assign. On an overspent envelope this becomes Cover overspending: take the shortfall from Ready to assign or another envelope
  • Withdraw — move money back to Unallocated (any envelope)
  • Revalue Balances — update exchange rates for foreign-currency balances
  • Archive — hide the envelope without deleting history. An archived envelope stops taking spending; to bring it back, open the Budget tab's ⋮ menu → Archived envelopes → Unarchive
  • Delete — permanently remove

An overspent envelope shows a red Cover button on its Budget card. After you save an expense, the confirmation says how much is left in its envelope — or how far it went over, with a Cover button.

When an envelope is overspent, it shows a red border. When a savings envelope reaches its goal, you'll see a confetti celebration!
Screenshot: Envelope detail showing balance hero card, fund button, and recent activity list

Categories

Categories organize your transactions. BudgetSeal uses a two-level system: parent categories (groups) and subcategories.

For example: Food & Dining (parent) → Groceries, Restaurants, Coffee & Snacks (subcategories).

Managing Categories

  1. Go to More → Categories
  2. Tap the + button to add a new category
  3. Enter a name, choose a parent (or make it top-level), pick an emoji icon and color
  4. Set it as Expense or Income type

Linking to Envelopes

Categories become powerful when linked to envelopes. When you spend on "Groceries" and that category is linked to your "Food" envelope, the expense automatically debits the envelope. Linking a parent category covers its subcategories too: if "Transport" is linked, spending on Fuel or Parking comes out of that envelope (unless a subcategory is linked to a different envelope).

Link categories when creating or editing an envelope (see Envelopes), or from the category side: long-press a category → Envelope to pick an envelope, unlink it, or create a new envelope named after the category. Linked categories show their envelope's name under them.

Category Icons

Pick from 120+ curated emojis organized by group, or type any emoji in the text field. The app shows the emoji on transaction tiles and category lists.

Screenshot: Categories screen showing parent groups with expandable subcategories

Funding Envelopes

When you receive income, it goes to your Unallocated pool — money that hasn't been assigned to any envelope yet. The goal is to distribute it all.

The gold Ready to assign banner at the top of the Budget tab is your unallocated money — tap Assign to fund envelopes. It shows your base currency amount prominently. If you have unallocated funds in other currencies, you'll see a "+ N other currencies" link — tap it (or the arrow) to expand and see the breakdown per currency.

Funding from the Dashboard

  1. Tap the Fund quick action on the Home tab
  2. You'll see all your envelopes with their current balances
  3. Enter how much to assign to each envelope — or use a preset: Quick Fill (up to each target), Same as last period, What I spent last period, or Clear. Fully funded envelopes still take extra money
  4. Tap Fund to confirm — all envelopes are funded together, or none if something fails

Funding a Single Envelope

  1. Go to the Budget tab
  2. Tap an envelope to open its detail screen
  3. Tap the Fund button on the balance card
  4. Enter the amount (or tap To target / All available) and tap Fund

Ordering Envelopes

Budget tab → ⋮ → Reorder envelopes: drag the handles to set the order the Budget tab and the funding screen use.

If you try to fund more than your unallocated balance, BudgetSeal will warn you. You can still proceed — your unallocated balance will go negative, which means you've assigned more than you have.

Period Transitions

At the start of each budget period (based on your period start day), periodic envelopes can be reset. You choose what happens to leftover balances:

  • Return to Unallocated — the leftover goes back to the pool
  • Carry Forward — keep the balance for the next period
  • Move to... — transfer the leftover to another envelope (e.g., savings)

An overspent envelope (below zero) can be covered from Ready to assign so it starts the new period at zero, or carry the overspending forward.

During the first week of a new period, a card on the Budget tab sums up the last one — how much you spent of what you budgeted — with a shortcut to fund the new period. Close it with ×.

Envelopes with Auto-reset enabled handle this automatically. Others will trigger a banner on the Budget tab showing exactly how many envelopes need your review (e.g., "3 envelopes need reset"). Tap Review to choose, for each one, whether its leftover returns to Unallocated, moves to another envelope, or stays. Once reviewed, the banner goes away until the next period starts; envelopes created during the period are not asked about until it ends.

Recurring Transactions

Set up transactions that repeat on a schedule — rent, salary, utility bills, etc.

Creating a Recurring Transaction

  1. Go to More → Recurring
  2. Tap the + button
  3. Fill in the details: type, title, amount, account, category
  4. Set the frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly) and interval
  5. Pick a start date
  6. Tap Save

Recurring transactions are auto-posted when they're due — when the app opens and whenever you come back to it. A message tells you how many were posted. You can enable or disable them with the toggle switch.

Bill Calendar

Go to More → Bill Calendar to see a monthly calendar view of all your upcoming bills. Tap a day to see what's due.

Upcoming Bills

Go to More → Upcoming Bills to see all bills sorted by due date with urgency indicators:

  • Indigo — Overdue
  • Blue — Due today, tomorrow, or in a few days

Tap a bill to Post now (pay it early — it's recorded today and the next date moves on), Skip this one (move to the next date without recording it), or Edit it. Subscriptions show the price that will actually be charged, including scheduled price changes.

Screenshot: Upcoming Bills screen showing bills with colored urgency badges

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are recurring transactions with extra tracking — price history, annual cost, and cancellation dates.

Creating a Subscription

  1. Go to More → Subscriptions
  2. Tap the + button — a "New Subscription" form opens directly
  3. Enter the service name, amount, frequency (weekly/monthly/yearly), and account
  4. Optionally set a start date and end date
  5. Tap Create

Subscription Detail

Tap a subscription to see:

  • Annual cost — calculated from the frequency and amount
  • Price history — a timeline of price changes (logged automatically when you edit the amount)
  • Past transactions — all payments made
  • Upcoming — next scheduled payment

You can Pause (disable) or set a Cancellation Date from the detail screen.

Planned Payments

Plan future one-time payments without affecting your current balances. Use this to prepare for next month's known expenses — rent, insurance, large purchases — so you can see what's coming before committing.

Creating a Planned Payment

  1. Go to More → Planned Payments
  2. Tap the + button
  3. Select the type (Expense, Income, or Transfer)
  4. Enter the amount, account, and category
  5. Choose the target month — defaults to next month
  6. Optionally pick an exact date within that month
  7. Tap Plan Payment

How Planned Payments Work

  • No balance impact — planned payments don't affect account balances, envelope balances, or reports until posted
  • Envelope preview — the Budget tab shows "$X planned" on envelopes that have planned expenses linked to their categories
  • Transaction list — planned payments appear dimmed at the top of the transaction list with a "Planned" badge, separate from real transactions

Posting (Confirming) a Payment

When you're ready to make the payment real:

  • Single — swipe right on a planned payment, or tap it and select "Post"
  • Bulk — tap "Post All" on a month header to post all planned payments for that month at once

Posting creates a real transaction through the budgeting engine — envelope balances update, account balances change, and it appears in reports.

Editing & Deleting

  • Edit — tap a planned payment to modify the amount, category, date, or other details
  • Delete — swipe left to remove a planned payment you no longer need

Templates

Templates save frequent transactions for one-tap reuse. Instead of filling in "Morning coffee, $4.50, Cash, Coffee & Snacks" every day, save it as a template.

Creating a Template

  1. Go to More → Templates
  2. Tap the + button
  3. Enter the transaction details: title, type, amount, account, category
  4. Tap Save Template

Using a Template

  • From the Templates screen — tap a template → Use template (long-press for Edit or Delete). A template in another currency gets the current exchange rate
  • From the Dashboard — your most-used templates appear in the Quick Templates section
  • When adding a transaction — tap the template icon in the form

Templates can be sorted by most used, A-Z, newest, or highest amount. You can also group them by type or category.

Goals & Loans

Track savings goals and money you've lent or borrowed — separate from your envelope budgeting.

Goals

A goal is a savings target: emergency fund, vacation, new laptop, etc.

  1. Go to More → Goals & Loans (also accessible from the Budget tab if you have existing goals)
  2. Tap Add → select Goal
  3. Enter a name, target amount, currency, and optional deadline
  4. Tap Save

To fund a goal:

  1. Open the goal — you'll see a progress card with the current amount and a progress bar
  2. Tap Add Funds (with a deadline, the summary shows how much you need each month to make it)
  3. Choose the account to save from
  4. Optionally pick a category for the transaction
  5. Enter the amount and confirm
  6. A real transaction is created — it appears in your transaction history

The detail screen shows a summary card (currency, deadline, remaining) and a payment history listing all recorded transactions. Payments are linked to the goal by an internal ID, so renaming the goal won't break the history. The progress is the total of these payments, so deleting a payment lowers it again. To edit the goal's settings, tap the 3-dot menu → Edit Settings.

Loans

Track money you've lent to someone or borrowed from someone.

  1. Tap Add → select Loan
  2. Enter the loan name, person, amount, and direction (I lent or I borrowed) — a hint below explains what each direction means
  3. Tap Save

To record payments:

  1. Open the loan
  2. Tap Record Payment
  3. Select the account, optionally a category, and enter the amount
  4. The payment appears in the payment history below
The category you pick for a payment is remembered for next time on the same objective. This keeps your payments consistently categorized.
Screenshot: Loan detail showing progress card, summary info, and payment history list

Travel Exchange

Going on a trip and need to exchange currency? BudgetSeal creates a temporary travel wallet to track your spending abroad.

Exchanging Money

  1. Go to More → Travel Exchange
  2. Select the account you're exchanging from
  3. Enter how much you're exchanging
  4. Choose the travel currency (the currency of your destination)
  5. Enter how much you received in the foreign currency
  6. Tap Exchange & Create Travel Wallet

A travel wallet account is created automatically. Use it to track your spending during the trip.

Coming Back

When you return and have leftover money:

  1. Go to Accounts → open the travel wallet
  2. Tap the 3-dot menu → Convert Back & Close
  3. Enter how much you got back in your base currency
  4. The travel wallet is archived
If you travel to the same country again, BudgetSeal will offer to reactivate the existing travel wallet instead of creating a new one.

Bill Splitter

Split a restaurant bill, shared groceries, or any group expense.

Three steps

  1. Go to More → Bill Splitter (or tap Split on the dashboard)
  2. Items — scan the receipt or add items by hand. Tap an amount to change it with the calculator; the split icon turns "3 × fries" into three items
  3. People — add who's splitting, then give each item to the people who had it (Everyone for shared dishes), or turn on Split evenly
  4. Review — each person's total, with their part of tax & service and tip. Amounts are rounded so they add up to the bill exactly

Who paid?

  • I paid the whole bill — the full bill is recorded from your account: your share plus one line per person ("Ali's part"). Each person also gets a loan in Goals & Loans for what they owe you; record their payment there when they pay you back
  • Someone else paid — nothing leaves your account now. A loan for what you owe them is added to Goals & Loans; recording your repayment there logs the expense
  • Everyone paid their own part — only your share is recorded

Tap Share split to send everyone's total to a group chat.

Tax & service and tip

Tax & service (a percentage or an amount) is split in proportion to what each person ordered. A percentage tip works the same way; a fixed tip is split evenly.

Receipt Scanning

  1. Tap Scan Receipt and take a photo or pick one from the gallery
  2. The app reads the receipt offline — no internet needed. It works with receipts in English, French and Arabic, among others
  3. Tap the lines you want to add. Discounts are added as negative amounts. Tax, service and total lines are recognised and aren't added as items
  4. The app compares your items with the receipt's total and tells you if something is missing. If the receipt lists tax or service separately and the numbers add up, it's filled in for you
The bill splitter supports cross-currency splits. Pick the bill's currency and the exchange rate is filled in from your saved rates — change it if your rate was different.

Reports

The Reports tab has four views to help you understand your finances. All tabs except Balance Sheet have a month navigator — use the left/right arrows (or swipe) to browse past months.

If your budget period doesn't start on the 1st, the calendar icon next to the title switches reports between Calendar months and Budget periods (e.g. 25 Sep – 24 Oct).

Overview

  • Monthly summary — income, expenses, and net for the selected month
  • Spending heatmap — a GitHub-style grid showing daily spending intensity. Green = income day, red = spending day, gray = no activity. Scroll left to see past months.
  • 6-Month Trend — a bar chart comparing income vs expenses over 6 months
  • Daily Pace — a cumulative line chart showing your spending pace vs typical month

Categories

  • Donut chart — the month's spending split by category, with the total in the middle
  • Category list — each category's amount, its share of the month (bar and %), number of transactions, and the change versus last month
  • Top spending / Top transactions — sort the list by amount or by number of transactions
  • Tap any category to see its transactions

Insights

  • Spending Velocity — your daily rate, projected total, and whether you're on pace
  • Biggest Expense — your largest single expense this month
  • Savings Rate — what percentage of income you're saving
  • Age of Money — how long money sits before being spent (see Age of Money)
  • Recurring Transactions — how much of your spending is committed

Balance Sheet

  • Net Worth — total of all accounts, grouped by currency
  • Assets — bank accounts, cash, digital wallets
  • Liabilities — credit cards
  • Compare — tap the compare icon to see how your balances changed vs. last week, last month, or any custom date

Export Report

Generate a printable HTML report for any month:

  1. Go to More → Settings → Import & Export → Export Report
  2. Select a month and year
  3. Tap Generate & Share
  4. Open the HTML file in a browser, then use Print → Save as PDF
Screenshot: Reports Overview tab showing monthly stats, spending heatmap, and 6-month trend chart

Age of Money

Age of Money tells you how many days your money sits in your accounts before you spend it. It replays all your income and expenses in order: each expense spends the oldest money still left (your accounts' opening balances count as the oldest), and the number shown is the average age of your last 10 expenses.

What the numbers mean:
  • Under 14 days — you're spending almost as fast as you earn
  • 14–30 days — you have a small cushion, getting ahead
  • 30–60 days — you're spending last month's income — great!
  • 60+ days — strong financial health, big safety net

The goal is to increase this number over time.

Your Age of Money appears on the dashboard status card and in the Reports → Insights tab.

Health Check

A diagnostic tool that verifies your data is consistent.

  1. Go to More → Settings & Customization → Health Check
  2. The app checks: balance invariants, orphan entries, backup status
  3. If issues are found, you can tap Repair Balances to fix discrepancies
  4. Purge Deleted permanently removes soft-deleted transactions
  5. Use the export button to share a diagnostic report
Run Health Check if something looks off — like a balance that doesn't match what you expect. It's safe to run anytime.

Cloud Sync

Sync your data to the cloud so you have a backup or can share with a partner. BudgetSeal uses a single JSON file — no server, no account registration.

Setting Up Sync

  1. Go to More → Settings & Customization → Cloud Sync
  2. Choose a provider:
    • Google Drive — sign in with your Google account
    • OneDrive — requires the OneDrive app installed
    • Dropbox — requires the Dropbox app installed
    • Local File — pick any file on your device
  3. Tap to connect — the app creates a sync file automatically. The Cloud Sync row in Settings then shows when you last synced (or that the last sync failed)

Once connected, the app syncs automatically when you open or close it. You can also tap Sync Now anytime.

Sync Encryption

For extra security, encrypt your sync file with a password:

  1. On the Cloud Sync screen, find the Sync Encryption card
  2. Tap Enable
  3. Enter a strong password and confirm it
  4. The next sync will encrypt the file with AES-256
If you share a household with someone, both devices must use the same sync password.

Sharing a Household

Share your BudgetSeal data with a partner:

  1. On the Cloud Sync screen, tap Share Household

Backup & Restore

Automatic Backups

  1. Go to More → Settings → Backup & Restore
  2. Enable Automatic Backups
  3. Set the frequency (every 6 hours to weekly)
  4. Set retention (how many backups to keep, 3 to 30)

Backups are saved locally on your device and run automatically when you open or close the app (if the frequency interval has passed). You can see all backups at the bottom of the screen and restore or delete any of them.

Manual Backup

Tap Export & Share to create a backup file and share it via any app (email, messaging, etc.).

Restoring

  • From a local backup — tap the restore icon next to any backup in the list
  • From a file — tap Restore from File and pick a .db file

The restored data takes over the next time the app starts. On Android, tap Close app and open it again; on iPhone, close the app from the app switcher and reopen it. Sync and automatic backups pause until then, so your old data is never uploaded over the restored one.

Always make a backup before restoring — the restore replaces ALL current data and cannot be undone.

Web Companion

Turn your phone into a local server and manage your budget from a laptop browser. Useful for fast data entry with a real keyboard.

How It Works

Your phone serves a web page over your local WiFi network. No internet needed — both devices just need to be on the same WiFi.

Starting the Server

  1. Go to More → Web Companion
  2. Set a 4-digit PIN (required for security)
  3. Tap Start Server
  4. A URL appears (e.g., http://192.168.1.5:7432)
  5. Open that URL on your laptop browser, or scan the QR code
  6. Enter the PIN on the web page

What You Can Do on the Web

The web page looks like the app — same colors (including the accent you picked), category icons, language and number format as your phone. Arabic shows right to left.

  • Home — Ready to assign, net worth, accounts, your envelopes and the latest transactions
  • Transactions — grouped by day; search, filter by type and month, load more. Click a row to edit it (including switching between expense, income and transfer); the row buttons duplicate or delete it, and a deleted transaction can be brought back with Undo for 5 seconds. Save & add another keeps the account and date for fast entry. Items of a split transaction are edited on the phone.
  • Add several — on the Transactions page, a grid for typing many expenses and incomes in one go: Tab moves across, Enter goes down a row (a new row copies the date, type and account above), Ctrl+Enter saves. Paste rows straight from a spreadsheet or bank statement — dates, amounts (a minus sign means an expense), category and account names are recognised, the rest becomes the title. Everything is saved together; if one row has a problem it's highlighted and nothing is saved until it's fixed. The draft stays if you switch pages.
  • Transfers between currencies — enter the amount sent and the amount received; the rate is worked out for you
  • Other currencies — an expense in a currency other than your base one can take a rate; leave it empty to use the latest rate your phone knows
  • Budget — every envelope with what's left, spent and its target; fund one with To target or All available. Funding more than you have ready to assign asks you to confirm. The ⇄ button on a card moves money to another envelope or back to Ready to assign; an overspent envelope shows a red Cover button that fills in the overspent amount and takes it from Ready to assign (or from the envelope with the most money when Ready to assign is empty). An envelope can only give what it holds.
  • Reports — browse months with the arrows: totals, savings rate, day-by-day chart, spending and income by category, biggest expenses
  • Accounts — net worth per currency; click an account to see its transactions; add an account (a negative balance for a card you owe on)
  • Categories — add, edit, recolor, or move a category inside another one
  • Recurring & Subscriptions — add (including recurring transfers), edit the amount, account, category, schedule and next date, pause or delete. Subscriptions show what they cost per month and per year; a price change goes into the price history.
  • Export transactions, recurring items or subscriptions as CSV (opens in Excel, accents and Arabic included)
  • Switch between light, dark and automatic mode

Changes made on the phone show up when you come back to the browser tab. If the phone goes out of reach, a banner tells you; the page reconnects by itself.

The page needs no internet: fonts, icons and charts all come from your phone.

Security

  • PIN is required — the page shows how many tries are left; 5 failed attempts lock the interface for 30 minutes
  • Only devices on the same WiFi network can connect
  • Server stops automatically after 6 hours
  • On Android, the server runs in the background (you can lock your phone)
  • On iOS, keep the app in the foreground — iOS doesn't allow background servers
Avoid using Web Companion on public WiFi (airports, hotels, cafes). The traffic is not encrypted, so someone on the same network could potentially see your data.

Import & Export

Go to More → Settings → Import & Export.

Import CSV

Import transactions from a bank CSV file. The app guides you through mapping columns to BudgetSeal fields. Dates can be 2026-03-04 or slash style (04/03/2026); the app works out day/month order from the whole file. Amounts can use either convention (1,234.56, 1.234,56, 12,50), and (5.00) counts as negative. Rows with an unreadable date or amount are skipped and counted, never dated today. Importing into a foreign-currency account uses its current exchange rate. Banks that split money in and out can map Debit and Credit columns instead of one Amount. Rows already in the app (same account, day, amount and type) are skipped, so overlapping statements don't double up, and rows without a category get one from past transactions with a matching title. Imported expenses in a category linked to an envelope spend from that envelope, just like manual entries.

Export CSV

Export all transactions as a CSV spreadsheet for use in Excel, Google Sheets, etc.

Export Report

Generate a formatted HTML report for a selected month. Open in a browser and print to PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between accounts and envelopes?

Accounts are where your money physically is (bank, wallet, credit card). Envelopes are how you plan to use it. You might have $5,000 across two bank accounts, but envelopes decide: $2,000 for rent, $500 for groceries, $300 for entertainment, and $2,200 ready to assign.

What does "Unallocated" mean?

It's money in your accounts that hasn't been assigned to any envelope yet. When you receive income, it goes to Unallocated. Your goal is to distribute it to envelopes so every dollar has a purpose.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Yes. Each account has its own currency. When you transfer between accounts in different currencies, an exchange rate field appears. Exchange rates are fetched automatically and cached for one hour; you can also set your own rate in Settings → Exchange rates. Reports and summaries show totals per currency — they're never mixed.

Paying in another currency: you can enter an expense in a different currency from the account (say €50 on a dollar card). It's saved in the account's currency using the rate you enter (or the latest known rate), and the original €50 is kept in the item's note — so account balances always stay in one currency. If no rate is known at all, the app asks you to enter one.

Envelopes and other currencies: spending is taken from the envelope in the currency you actually paid in. If a dollar envelope pays for something in pounds from a pound account, the envelope shows a small "other currency" debt in pounds until you fund it in pounds.

Recurring bills and planned payments in another currency use the latest rate when they're posted (recurring bills use the last rate saved on your device, so the app never waits for the internet at start-up). A recurring transfer between two currencies waits until a rate is known.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. Cloud sync is optional and uses your own Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox — BudgetSeal never sees your data. There are no servers, no accounts, and no tracking.

What happens if I delete a transaction?

Deleted transactions are soft-deleted — they're hidden but not permanently removed. Any envelope deductions are reversed. You can permanently purge them from Health Check.

Can two people share a budget?

Yes. Set up Cloud Sync with Google Drive on one device, then share the household with your partner. Both devices sync to the same file. Changes merge automatically — the newer edit wins.

What if I overspend an envelope?

The envelope goes negative and shows a red indicator. Your transaction still saves — BudgetSeal never blocks spending. It's a signal to either fund the envelope more or spend less.

How do I change my base currency?

Go to More → Settings & Customization → Base Currency. Note: this changes the display currency for summaries and reports. Existing transactions keep their original currency.

My notifications aren't working

Make sure notifications are enabled in your device settings for BudgetSeal. On Android 13+, you need to grant notification permission explicitly. Go to Settings → Notifications to configure daily reminders and bill alerts. BudgetSeal asks for the permission the first time you open your budget. Alerts are checked when the app opens or comes back, at most once a day each; the daily reminder skips days you've already logged something.

Home screen widget (Android)

Add the BudgetSeal widget from your launcher's widget list. It shows your Ready to assign amount and what you've spent today, updated each time you leave the app. Tap it to open BudgetSeal.
