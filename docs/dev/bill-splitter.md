# Bill Splitter & receipt OCR

Developer notes moved out of CLAUDE.md (Oct 2026). Read before changing this area; update when you change it.

## Bill Splitter

`lib/features/transactions/bill_splitter_screen.dart` — More > Bill Splitter or the Split quick action (`/bill-splitter`, premium). **One screen** (Oct 2026 redesign): the body is one flat ListView, the controls live in `Scaffold.bottomNavigationBar` (a `Material` + `Column(mainAxisSize: min)` — no `Expanded` in it).

- **Bill card** (decluttered Oct 30 2026 — on the A02 at display zoom + 110% text the old card filled the whole view): total (`_subtotalEntered`, calculator; in By item it is the items' sum and not tappable), currency pill (`CurrencySheet`, `_setBillCurrency` prefills the rate with `rateToBaseOrOne`); cross-currency: one line "≈ $25.17 · 1 USD = 90,000 LBP ✎" (`_rateText`; "Set the exchange rate" when none) that opens the **rate sheet** (`_editRate`: field, direction swap, ≈ base, the receipt's rate) and, when the receipt prints a different rate, "Use the receipt's rate: 90,000" under it (one tap); tip as one full-width `_Segments` row No tip/10%/15%/Custom (`_tipPercent` proportional, Custom = `_tipAmount` split evenly; a line "subtotal + tip …" shows only when there's a tip); optional "+ Tax or service charge" (`_taxOn`, percent or amount, proportional) that becomes one chip "Tax or service · LBP 150,000 ×" (tap = edit, × = drop). "Split in USD" lives on the totals page only. Lebanon has tips, not service — so service is opt-in.
- **Who's in** + `_Segments` Evenly | By item (`_SplitMode`). `_people.first` is the user ("Me", shown "You"), never removable. Recent people = loan `contactName`s (`objectivesProvider`). **Evenly**: rows with live shares; tapping an amount sets it by hand (`_fixed`, the others split the rest, the last free person can't be fixed); row tap → set/reset/remove sheet. **By item**: `_BillItem.people` empty = everyone; pick a person in the bottom strip (`_painting`, the banner scrolls into view) and tap items to toggle them (`_toggleItem`; someone must keep each item) — or **All / None** in the banner (`_pickAllItems`, Oct 30 2026, for long bills; with two or more people): All puts them on every item, None takes them off every item, and an item only they had goes back to everyone (`billItemsBackToEveryone` SnackBar with Undo); tapping an item otherwise edits it (name, calculator amount, "Split into N" for a scanned quantity, delete). Removing a person whose items were theirs alone makes those items everyone's (SnackBar with Undo; Undo restores every item's people as they were, shared items too).
- `_shares()` → per-person totals (evenly: fixed + rest; by item: item shares + proportional tax + tip), `_rounded()` = largest remainder to the rounded total, keeping hand-set shares exact.
- **Split into N** (`_splitItem`): an item with `qty > 1` (read from the receipt — leading "3 X", inline "2 @ 4.50", trailing 2–20 — or set with the − / + Quantity stepper in the item dialog; the amount is the line total, "×N" is kept in the name) shows "Split into 3" as accent text under the row's amount (not while picking a person) and in its edit dialog; it becomes N items of amount/N, "(1/3)…", keeping the item's people. Without it the line is shared evenly by its owners regardless of the quantity.
- **Item amounts** (`_fmtItem`): a currency written as a spaced prefix (`currencyHasSpacedPrefix`: "LBP 180,000") is left off — the bill card names it; a tight symbol ("$12.50") stays. Same in the "Everyone · … each" line.
- **Editing bar** (`_editBar`): By-item person strip (`_PersonChip`, width follows the amount) + **See totals** (needs a total) → `_reviewing = true`. Just you: strip and a compact See totals share one row; with others the strip gets its own row (on a narrow phone a shared row hid people behind the button).
- **Totals page** (`_totalsPage`, Oct 25 2026; same State, not a route, so nothing is lost; its own flat ListView with a `PageStorageKey` so it opens at the top): bill total + "≈ base · 1 USD = 90,000 LBP" + the Split-in switch; bill breakdown when there's a tip or tax (`_BreakdownRow`: Before tip = `_subtotal`, tax, tip, bill currency); "Each person pays" card — per person a name/amount row, then "$26.94 + tip $2.70" (`_tipParts()`: tip in proportion to each share Evenly, tip % of what they had By item, evenly for a custom tip; rounded to add up to the tip, converted with `_payShares`; before tip = share − tip part, so equal shares can differ by a cent before tip), then the bill-currency amount when splitting in base, each on its own line (fits at 360 dp with large text); Paid by pill (`_pickPayer`: You / each person / Each their own) + consequence ("Sam owes you $26.94"), From account (`LastUsedService.defaultAccount`, `_pickAccount`) + category (`CategorySheet`) in a Wrap; bar: Save (label per mode — `billSaveAmount` when you paid, since the account is shown above), Edit split, Share. Back (AppBar or system, `PopScope(canPop: false)`) returns to editing.
- **Split in base** (`_splitInBase`, prefs `bill_split_in_base`; shown when the bill currency ≠ base and a real rate is set, on the bill card and the totals page): `_payShares()` converts each share at the bill rate and rounds with `_rounded(factor: base decimals)` so they add up to the converted total; `_fmtPay`/`_payCurrency` for shares, what's owed, Share text, the saved sheet's rows and the **loans** (`_createLoans` uses `_payCurrency`). Money leaving the account stays in the bill currency (Save label, `billSavedFrom/Each`, the recorded expense at `_exchangeRate`). A share typed by hand is in the pay currency and stored back in the bill's (÷ rate). The auto-filled rate is the rounded one shown (`roundRate`: 89,881 → 90,000), so the maths match the field.
- **Save** records directly (no add form; with no live account at all it says `billNeedAccount` instead of opening an empty picker): `me` → `recordTransaction` expense with the user's share (category) + one `billLinePart` line per person, then a `lent` loan each; `other` → a `borrowed` loan to the payer only; `each` → the user's share only. Then `_showSaved()` sheet: who owes what (per-person send via SharePlus), Share split, Done (pops) and Undo (`_undo`: deletes the payment, soft-deletes the loans, keeps the bill on screen).
- **Scan** (camera in the app bar, or the empty By-item state): with items already entered, a scan with no priced line keeps them (`billScanKeptItems`) and one with prices asks first (`billReplaceItemsTitle`); otherwise every priced line becomes an item and the mode switches to By item; `_receiptMatches` compares items with the receipt total (or the total typed before itemizing): a match is a quiet "✓ Matches the receipt" beside Add item, a mismatch the tinted `_receiptCheck` card; `_unpricedLines` lists item lines read without a price (tap → calculator → item). Tax/service is prefilled when items + tax match the receipt total.
- Placeholders with two or more values (`billSaveFrom`, `billOwesYouOne`, `billSavedFrom`…) declare their order in app_en.arb — without metadata gen-l10n sorts them alphabetically and the arguments land in the wrong slots.
- OCR (`lib/shared/utils/ocr_service.dart`, ML Kit Latin script, offline — Arabic script can't be read, parked): `scanReceipt(path, currency:)` only collects lines (+ angle and height from `cornerPoints`); `parseLines()` (pure, tested) does the rest: straightens the photo (median line slope), groups lines into rows by straightened centre (½ line height, never side-by-side overlaps), tidies numbers (`_fixDigits`: O→0 and I/l→1 inside numbers only; `_tidyNumbers`: "1,250, 000"), classifies (`OcrLineKind`, EN/FR/AR/ES/DE keywords; date/time lines are `other`), then: nothing after the grand total is an item; bare whole-number prices (`weakPrice`) above the first real price or off the price column aren't prices; quantity lines ("3 x 150,000  450,000") and a lone price row go to the priceless name above; a priceless text row under an item is the rest of its name (within 2.5 line heights); priceless rows indented under a priced item are its parts (a meal's "1 Regular Fries", "NO Ice" — the first one joins the name only when it has no leading count); above the first priced item nothing is an item or tax (shop name, "VAT no"); "Total (incl VAT)"/TTC is a total, other included-VAT lines and "VAT no"/"Tax invoice" are not tax; delivery is a charge (tax kind → prefilled as tax/service); a price glued to a code ("MDS-8865", "122732-601") isn't a price; a total/tax label with its amount on the row below ("TOTAL DUE" / "6300000.00") takes it; 5+ ungrouped digits before one separator = decimal point ("6300000.000"); a row ending in a zero price ("1 W/Regular 0.00") is not an item; a row starting with a count never joins the name above; a printed currency that contradicts the amounts ("USD 70.00" under an LBP total) is an equivalent and ignored; `_matchTotal` multiplies one suspicious 2-decimal price by 10/100/1000 when that alone makes the items match the total ("650,00" → 650,000). Header numbers (Oct 24 2026, Why Cook delivery slip): a bare run of digits with no separator, currency or suffix ("20035") is a weak price, so step 6 drops it above the first real price or off the price column; a number with a leading zero ("01600000" — phone numbers) is never a price, on a line or alone (`_lonePrice`); "Order Nb 20035" / "CHECK # 1600000" / "Ref 8000000" / "Table 12" — or several on one line, "CHECK # 105165 TABLE #1" (`_reference`, word boundary `` after the keyword) — are other; a count, a year and a price ("1 2014  2700000", wines by vintage — Beit Bhamdoun receipt, Oct 26 2026) is an item named after the year (`_yearItem` keeps it out of `_isNoise`; `_finishItem` takes the year as the name); a zero price may carry a suffix ("0T"). "LL" as a whole word names LBP. Leading codes are stripped only when they contain a digit. A price of a billion or more (`OcrService.maxReceiptPrice`, the engine's line limit) is not a price. Delivery slips (Oct 30 2026, real OmegaPOS slip in the test): a **column-header row** ("Qty Description Total", `_isTableHeader`: only column words, two or more, no digits — it used to read as a total) opens the table (step 4c): every item/tax/discount above it is other (customer name, company, phones, street, "Flr: 4", the shop's phone beside "…iye", the "Delivery" title), and the "header above the first priced item" rule starts at the table instead, so a "Delivery Charge" listed before the first dish stays a charge. When too few prices are "strong" (every price bare, "150000"), step 6 finds the price column from all priced rows. A leading code is stripped only when it's capitals and digits not starting with a word (`(?![A-Z]{4})`, case-sensitive) — "Comb0300g fries" (Combo, O read as 0) keeps its name. **Receipt rate**: `OcrService.printedRate(result, bill:, other:)` — totals that name their currency (`_totalCurrency`: LL/L.L/LBP/ل.ل, $/USD, €/EUR, an ISO code) give the shop's rate (25.17 / 2,265,000); the splitter shows "Use the receipt's rate: 90,000" under the rate line and "The receipt uses 1 USD = 90,000 LBP — use it" in the rate sheet (`_receiptRateShown`, rounded with `roundRate`, hidden once it's the rate in use). The bill currency settles "12.500" (12.5 in a 3-decimal currency, else 12,500). Android gets the photo from Google's document scanner (`google_mlkit_document_scanner`: edge crop, flatten, clean-up, gallery import; Play-services module downloaded on first use) and falls back to the camera/gallery sheet when it can't start; iOS uses the sheet. **Receipt currency**: `OcrService.receiptCurrency(result, billCurrency:, userCurrencies:)` (pure, tested) — a currency the receipt names (ISO code of one of the user's currencies, or ل.ل/L.L/€/£/د.إ) → the splitter switches at once with an Undo SnackBar (`_checkReceiptCurrency`); else whole-thousand amounts on a small-unit bill → the user's one `largeUnitCurrencies` currency (several → the shop's country, `_countryHints`), cents on a large-unit bill → the one other currency → `_currencyHint` line under the total ("Looks like LBP — switch?"). Only the base + account currencies are ever suggested. `OcrResult.receiptTotal` (largest total) drives the step-1 total check; `taxTotal` prefills tax when items + tax match the receipt total. Tests: `test/features/ocr_service_test.dart` (synthetic tilted receipts + real ML Kit scans, incl. a McDonald's Lebanon receipt). To capture a new real receipt: temporarily `debugPrint` each `OcrRawLine` (text, rect, angle, height) in `scanReceipt`, scan it on the emulator (gallery fallback) and turn the log into a test.
- Dialogs: discard ("Keep editing" | red "Discard") and rate-not-set ("Go back" | "Save anyway") use `showConfirmDialog`; the "From" account is the shared `showAccountPickerSheet()`; "Who's in" is a `SectionHeader` with the mode switch as its trailing widget.
